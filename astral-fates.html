THE WORLD

Grief is what
makes us human.

Every 800 years, a calamity named the Dark Star comes to wreak havoc on the world. Astral dieties lend their powers to fighters who have the will to become the strongest. Some are unable to posess that power, some are consumed by it. Only those who are able to overcome their grief and accept themselves will they be able to save their home.
